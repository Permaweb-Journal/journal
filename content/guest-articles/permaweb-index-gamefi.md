## I. Introduction

The Permaweb Index has added two new projects: StarGrid Battle Tactics’ Space Money $SMONEY and ArcAO’s $GAME token. These projects bring cutting-edge gaming technology to the permaweb and are pioneering the GameFi narrative on AO.

Now, the community can support them by allocating AO yield to $SMONEY and $GAME through the Permaweb Index. This article explores both projects and how the Permaweb Index works.

## II. What is StarGrid Battle Tactics?

![Stargrid Welcome Screen](https://permaweb-journal.arweave.net/static/images/stargrid.jpeg)

StarGrid Battle Tactics is a 1v1 turn-based strategy game built entirely on AO. Every interaction is a transaction, permanently stored on Arweave.

Players get four ranked matches per day. By staking $DAI, they can unlock additional daily matches.

And you’ll recognize the characters: play as Vitalik Beauticorn, Space Emperor Tromp, Samurai BZ, and Optimos Eloon.

### $SMONEY Overview

![Stargrid Permaweb Index](https://permaweb-journal.arweave.net/static/images/stargrid-pi.png)

- **Earned in Ranked Matches**: Win games to earn $SMONEY
- **Max Supply**: 20,000,000,000
- **Distribution**:
  - 70%: Earned through ranked matches
  - 15%: Mintable via the Permaweb Index
  - 7%: Airdropped
  - 5%: Reserved for DEX liquidity
  - 3%: Allocated to marketing, ambassadors, and partners

StarGrid combines fast-paced gameplay with crypto-native rewards, using AO and Arweave to immutably record every match outcome.

Play StarGrid [here](https://stargrid.ar.io/).

## III. What is ArcAO?

![RuneRealm Welcome Screen](https://permaweb-journal.arweave.net/static/images/runerealm-header.png)

ArcAO is building provably fair games and gaming infrastructure on AO. Its titles include [Satoshi’s Palace](https://satoshispalace.casino/) and [Rune Realm](https://runerealm-onchain_game.ar.io/), powered by [RandAO](https://www.randao.net/), a verifiable randomness protocol that ensures fairness in game outcomes.

### $GAME Overview

![ArcAO Permaweb Index](https://permaweb-journal.arweave.net/static/images/arcao-pi.png)

- **Fair Launch**: 70% of 1B tokens distributed transparently
- **No VC Allocation**: 0% to private investors
- **Utility**: Funds GameFi projects and marketing

ArcAO aims to bootstrap AO as the GameFi chain, structured around three pillars:

1.  **Accelerator Program**: Funds early-stage GameFi projects in exchange for equity and community support.
2.  **Ambassador Program**: A distributed content team promotes games across the permaweb. The last cohort included 80 creators from over 500 applicants.
3.  **Compounding Investment Pool**: Profits from past accelerations are reinvested into future GameFi projects.

Learn more about $GAME [here](https://docs-arcao_game.openar.dev/docs/investors/overview).

## IV. What is the Permaweb Index?

![Permaweb Index Delegate UI](https://permaweb-journal.arweave.net/static/images/delegate-ui.png)

The Permaweb Index is a permissionless ecosystem funding mechanism. It enables users to passively support ecosystem projects while giving builders fair, non-VC funding options.

The **PI token** represents a diversified allocation across the ecosystem:

- 33.3% Arweave ($AR)
- 33.3% AO
- 33.3% fair launch projects like $SMONEY and $GAME

## V. How to allocate AO yield to the Permaewb Index

AO yield is customizable. On the [Delegate](https://ao.arweave.net/#/delegate/) page, users can allocate their earned AO to:

- **PI**: A balanced index across AO, AR, and ecosystem tokens
- **AO**: Retain all yield in AO
- **AR**: Convert AO yield into Arweave for permanent storage (coming soon)

### Allocating to fair launch tokens

![Permaweb Index Delegate UI](https://permaweb-journal.arweave.net/static/images/delegate-ui2.png)

To support $SMONEY or $GAME directly:

- Go to the **Delegate** tab
- Click **Details & Add** under your chosen project
- Adjust your allocation
- Click **Save Changes**

You’ll now passively earn project tokens while supporting their growth.

By allocating yield to $SMONEY and $GAME, users don’t just earn tokens, they actively fund the next generation of decentralized games and infrastructure.
